# The bottles

**Hands-off, room temperature: nothing on the robot can spoil.** 16 pumps → 28 drinks.

* **Spirits and liqueurs** keep for years once opened.
* **No juice.** Bar flavor syrups (Monin / Torani style) are made to sit open at room
  temperature; the robot mixes them with water to make "orange", "cranberry",
  "pineapple" and "lime" (e.g. 1 part orange syrup + 5 parts water).
* **Water jug** is the only refill besides empty bottles.
* Drinks come out at **room temperature** (add ice if you like).

## Honest trade-offs
* Syrup + water tastes **sweeter and less fresh** than real juice. Adjust the ratios
  in `config.yaml` (`made_from`) after your first tasting; drinks that also use cane
  syrup may want less of it.
* "Lemon" drinks use lime syrup.
* If you ever want real juice for a party, put it on a pump and the app uses it
  instead of the syrup mix automatically.

## Maintenance (the whole list)
* Replace bottles when empty (the Admin page will show what's low in a future version).
* Refresh the water jug about weekly.
* About once a month, or before a long break: run water through all pumps
  (Admin → Clean) so sugar doesn't get sticky in the tubes.

## Not on the menu
| Drink | Why |
|---|---|
| Anything with soda (Rum & Coke, G&T, Mule…) | Fizz goes flat in pumps — off by default |
| White Russian, Mudslide, Piña Colada | Cream/dairy spoils |
| Manhattan, Martini, Negroni | Vermouth goes stale after opening |
| Gin drinks | Gin swapped out (its recipes return if you put gin on a pump) |
